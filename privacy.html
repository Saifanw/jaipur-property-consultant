Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

When you send an enquiry, list a property, ask a question or write a review, we collect the details you type: name, mobile number, area, property details, budget, any photos you upload, and your message. We also collect basic visit data (pages viewed, device type, approximate city) to understand how the website is used.

Why we use it

We use your details to call or message you about properties, to match your requirement with suitable properties, to list your property if you asked us to, and to improve the website. We do not sell your details.

What is shown publicly

Your mobile number and exact address are never published. Property details, photos and reviews appear on the website only after our team approves them and only if you agreed when submitting.

Who processes the data

The website uses Google Firebase (database and photo storage), Google Analytics 4 (visit statistics) and Cloudflare (hosting). These providers process data on our behalf under their own security terms. Approximate location is taken from the connection by Cloudflare and no IP address is stored by us.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and customer records while they are useful for your property search or our legal and accounting needs. You can ask us to delete your details at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Message or call us on the number below and we will act on it.
